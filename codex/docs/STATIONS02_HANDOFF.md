## What is implemented

`scripts/ui/eito/eito_layer.gd` routes **17 applicable board types** to dedicated native Godot `Control` scripts in `scripts/ui/eito/`: cargo inspection scale, pallet mover, maintenance trolley, cylinder rack, legitimate supply stores, air duct, air filter, CO2 scrubber, oxygen rack, coolant pump, turbine, valve manifold, helm, communications, planning table, nutrient pump check, and seedling rack check. Multiple prop instances share a board type while receiving their own applicable model data. The tied-bundle prototype board is intentionally absent because that prop is hiding only. There is a generic fallback for content not covered by the artifact.

These boards are connected to existing mission actions, inventory/consumable rules, healthy-reading timers, story gates, and acknowledgement timing. Native hit regions support mouse-driven sequences. The original extracted Pixelify Sans, Silkscreen, VT323 fonts and station graphics/materials are under `art/ui/fonts/` and `art/ui/eito/stations02/`; the visuals are drawn through `scripts/ui/eito/eito_ui.gd`, `eito_screen.gd`, `artifact_station_screen.gd`, and the dedicated panels. New PNGs have Godot `.res` companions generated by `tools/convert_station_materials.gd` because runtime imported PNG sampling was unreliable in the test renderer.

H-key hiding is integrated into the existing controller in `scripts/player/player_controller.gd` and `scripts/ship/ship_mission_walkthrough.gd`. The four registered places are the cargo locker, cargo tied-box bundle (one object), and two engine lockers. Entry/exit prompts, route and collision checks, blocked-exit feedback, occupancy, equipment visibility, and save/load recovery are implemented. Hiding props do not open storage interfaces. Locker movement uses the existing pressure-door audio at restrained volume; no suitable fabric/rope sound was available for the bundle. See `docs/stations02_mapping.md` for the complete gameplay mapping and telemetry limitations.

Recent fidelity fixes include: communications switch/teletype and post-send state; oxygen rack's unsupported per-tank gauges removed; air-filter lock tint, FIT dial and captions; trolley's previously doubled front-control graphic separated into a clean background; valve-manifold animated flow and locktag; scrubber bed-detail/current-data labels; air-duct fan/gauge timing and unknown ΔP display; pallet mover storage destination relabelled `TROLLEY`. These changes are in the uncommitted working tree. The latest `TROLLEY` label edit has **not** been recaptured visually.
