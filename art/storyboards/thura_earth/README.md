# Thura Min: Earth Years

An eight-frame backstory storyboard for Dr. Thura Min, following the character history in the project narrative. The images are standalone concept frames in a cinematic 2D pixel-art treatment.

1. **Yangon rooftop** — Young Thura studies the constellations with his mother during a power cut. She teaches him that fear can be listened to.
2. **Radio lessons** — His father shows him how to diagnose a machine by listening to it.
3. **The scholarship** — A university acceptance and scholarship open the way to planetary geology and robotics.
4. **The first setback** — Thura sits with the disappointment of an early astronaut assessment failure.
5. **Trying again** — He returns to training and prepares to make another attempt.
6. **Neutral buoyancy** — He rehearses spacecraft repair in an underwater training facility.
7. **Selected for the mission** — Thura stands before the research spacecraft in his flight suit.
8. **May's paper star** — His younger sister gives him the star folded from one of their mother's old lesson books.
9. **Thadingyut star memory** — At the festival, young Thura plays with a sparkler, then asks his mother about a bright star. The cutscene uses three distinct shots: the rooftop celebration, their close family moment, and the mother pointing to the star.

These are visual story concepts; they are not currently wired into a game scene.

Expanded three-frame sequences for the scholarship, neutral-buoyancy training, and May farewell scenes are listed in [sequences/README.md](sequences/README.md).
